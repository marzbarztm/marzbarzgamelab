HOW TO PLAY

SWAT FLIES to build score and combos.

KILL MAGGOTS FAST. Any maggot left alive for 3 seconds splits into 3 and spreads across the screen.

FLY FRENZY is a high-speed score rush packed with flies.

MAGGOT RUSH shifts the pressure to scattered, multiplying maggots.

POWER-UPS: Love Sick, Rotten Pollination, Maggot Magnet, and Filthy Duality. You may store them together, but only one can be active at a time.

Keep the Rot Meter from filling. Survive the infestation and push your high score.
