Rotted Threadz

High-end streetwear from a mind that finds beauty in everything unfamiliar. Founded by Jaime, Rotted Threadz channels decay, symbolism, memory, and South Texas grit into a brand that feels considered, personal, and unmistakably its own.

Inside the mind

Not clean.
Not careless.

Rotted Threadz lives in the space between luxury and decay.

At its core, Rotted Threadz is a refined streetwear brand shaped by Jaime’s point of view—dark, intentional, and deeply personal. The references are there, but they are handled with restraint: rot, flies, maggots, superstition, and the uneasy beauty found in things most people overlook.

Nothing feels overworked. Every piece leans on mood, texture, and identity, giving the brand a quieter confidence that stands out without forcing itself to.

Created in South Texas.Designed for people who were never trying to blend in.
